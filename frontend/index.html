Haripur's Pride on Main GT Road

More Than Just Fast Food — It’s Haripur’s Gathering Spot

Conveniently situated at Akhtar Nawaz Khan Plaza near Sabzi Mandi (right opposite Nishat Mandi Mor), Bella’s was built to answer one simple question: "Where can Haripur families and late-night highway travelers get honest, hot, high-quality fast food?"

Whether you’re heading back from Abbottabad or Islamabad on the GT Road, catching up with friends after university, or treating your family on the weekend, we provide comfortable air-conditioned seating, generous portion sizes, and service that treats you like family.

Dedicated Family Hall: Clean, comfortable, respectful dining ambiance for families.

GT Road Car Takeaway: Call ahead on 0336-1131166 and have your hot food ready to pick up.
